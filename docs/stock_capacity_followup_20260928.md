with fixed 20% weights, showed a larger improvement than the stock-only
two-position cap. This is a post-screen diagnostic, not new OOS evidence.

Before drawing a conclusion, replay all five shared capacities, retaining 20%
weights, three-stock concurrency and two stock entries per UTC day. Compare
unified admission with matched two-stage preselection. Apply the already priced
0/5/10-minute stock entry delays. Do not change crypto predictions, outcomes,
price assumptions, thresholds or cooldowns. Stress costs are stocks 68bp and
crypto 16bp. Test the original historical window without funding, and July-
August with actual stock funding coverage; crypto funding remains omitted.

All candidate identities, time conventions and source hashes must be retained.
Report every capacity, monthly returns, marked risk and contribution by sleeve.
The earlier bootstrap result cannot certify live crypto features or future
alpha. A result that only works with the frozen crypto comparator remains an
allocation research candidate; it does not authorize a live switch.
